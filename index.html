UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH · MAY–AUGUST 2026

Gaussian-Type Integrals: Theory, Computation, and Applications

Research Supervisor: Professor Stephanie Wisnowski

  • Researched generalized Gaussian integrals, investigating their dependence on the exponent and connections to the Gamma function.
  • Derived analytical expressions and examined special cases, asymptotic behavior, and convergence properties.
  • Identified a nontrivial minimum near n = 2.16623, showing that the classical Gaussian case (n = 2) lies close to the minimizing exponent.
  • Evaluated numerical integration methods, including the Trapezoidal and Simpson's Rules, comparing numerical approximations with analytical results.
  • Selected to present research at the January 2027 Joint Mathematics Meetings (JMM) through the AMS–PME Undergraduate Poster Session.
